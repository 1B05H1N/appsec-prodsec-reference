# Career outlook (context, not advice)

Security hiring moves in cycles. Teams shrink, re-org, or freeze headcount while expectations for the same roles stay high. Budgets are often tight: fewer seats for training and conferences, more pressure to show ROI on tools, and longer approval paths for anything that costs money.

If you are job searching, maintaining a program on a shoestring, or mentoring someone who is, it helps to assume:

- **Time and energy are finite** - prioritize depth where it matters for your goals, not every possible certification or side project.
- **Your situation is not a moral verdict** - slow searches and constrained budgets are structural as often as they are personal.
- **This repository does not replace** recruiters, mentors, compensation data, or labor law. It is reference material written in public.

For resume structure and an anonymized example aligned to common security-leadership patterns, see [resume-guide-and-generic-example.md](resume-guide-and-generic-example.md).
